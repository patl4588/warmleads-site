    STRAIGHT ANSWERS

    Questions owners ask.

    Still unsure? Book a free consultation

    What counts as a qualified lead?

    A matched contact looks right on paper. A qualified lead fits your services and area and has shown interest. A booked appointment has an agreed date and time. We agree the exact qualification and billing criteria with you before launch.

    Where does the AI run?

    Runs on our local AI: our AI runs on our own computers. No technical setup for you. Your approved emails still travel through your own email provider.

    Do you need my password?

    No. Never send us your email password. The signup flow is designed for Google or Microsoft authorization. You control the connection and can disconnect it. Company Microsoft 365 accounts may need an administrator’s approval.

    When do I pay?

    No card to start. Your first 2 weeks are free: setup, prospecting, writing and follow-ups. After that, the service is $300/month flat, month to month. Cancel before the 2 weeks are up and you pay nothing. There are no per-lead fees or commissions.

    What happens after 2 weeks?

    If you want to keep going, add a card and it’s $300/month, month to month. If not, you pay nothing. Either way, we agree up front what counts as a qualified lead for your business.

    Will this hurt my email reputation?

    We use published contacts, address checks, measured daily batches and bounce monitoring to reduce risk. Bad addresses are suppressed and sending pauses if bounces climb. No outreach service can guarantee inbox placement or zero reputation risk.

    Where do the contacts come from?

    Public business sources. Scouts look for businesses in your service area, the Prospect Agent finds who makes the decision, and Bounce Guard checks the published address. We do not guess email addresses.

    What if my customer list is a mess?

    Bring it. A CSV export from your CRM, email tool or accounting system is a starting point. Include past customers, leads that never signed and your do-not-contact list. The agents also find new prospects.

    Who handles the replies?

    Your agents handle routine replies, out-of-office messages, redirects and opt-outs. Real interest goes to the person you choose, with the full email thread. Your office takes the conversation from there and wins the work.

    How much of my time does setup take?

    Not much. Tell us about your business, connect your email and approve the messages. We do the rest. Mailbox authorization, list readiness and any IT review can affect timing, so this is not a promise of instant launch.

    Can I pause or cancel?

    Yes. You can pause sending, and the service is month to month. If you leave, you keep your cleaned list, scored prospects and email sequences.

    Do you work with my competitors?

    Ask us about your trade and territory before starting. We’ll explain the applicable arrangement. Don’t assume territory exclusivity unless it is agreed in writing.
